# Tax Loss Harvesting Review

Review supplied holdings and cost-basis information to identify positions with unrealized losses, note recent or expected purchases that may raise wash-sale questions, and summarize the factual tax-lot information for qualified professional review. Do not recommend or execute transactions, do not provide tax advice, and flag jurisdiction-specific questions for a tax professional.

## Non-negotiable standards

- Treat the work as analyst work product for human review, not as an authorization to trade, transact, approve, post, bind risk, or provide legal/tax/accounting advice.
- Separate facts, assumptions, calculations, and judgments. Label estimates and scenario assumptions explicitly.
- Never invent a missing figure. If required cost-basis, holding-period, transaction-history, or jurisdiction data is unavailable, state the gap.
- Reconcile units, currencies, dates, and account identifiers before comparing figures.
- Run explicit arithmetic and consistency checks before presenting results.
